# steering

design notes/ rules 

- V.3.2 Steering page 21
- steering wheel must be below the front loop at any steering angle
- mechanical link to wheels no steer by wire
- postive stops in steering to prevent tires from touching the car
- less than 7 degrees of free play
    - asi qr steering wheel standard is 2 degrees of play so will nneed to plan for less than 5 degrees
- steering fasteners are critical fasteners t.8.2
- Spherical rod ends and spherical bearings in the steering must meet V.3.1.5 above
- steering wheel
    - has to use qd fitting
    - continuous perimeter to wheel near circular or oval (cannot be a figure 8)
    - can be removed by driver when seated with gloves on
