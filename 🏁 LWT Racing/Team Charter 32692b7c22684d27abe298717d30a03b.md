# Formula SAE Team Charter

## Lake Washington Institute of Technology | 2026–2027

### Purpose

Our goal is to design, build, and compete with a Formula SAE car that passes technical inspection, completes dynamic events, and reflects sound engineering decisions under real constraints. Our first priority is finishing a safe, functional car; performance improvements come after reliability and compliance.

### Development Approach

We follow a two-stage process:

- **Test Chassis ("Mule"):** A pre-rules rolling platform used to validate suspension, steering, braking, drivetrain packaging, and fabrication methods. The mule is not rules-compliant and will not compete.
- **Competition Car:** Designed after rules release with a rapid architecture freeze, carrying forward validated concepts and optimizing only after reliability is proven.

### Engineering Values

- Simplicity over cleverness
- Predictable behavior over aggressive targets
- Manufacturability as a design requirement
- Testing and documentation as part of engineering
- Learning from mistakes is expected

### Team Structure & Ownership

Rather than fixed titles, we use system ownership. Each major system has a clearly responsible owner for design intent, build coordination, and documentation. Roles may evolve as skills and team size change, but ownership remains clear.

### Decision-Making

Decisions are guided by safety, reliability, rule compliance, manufacturability, and evidence from analysis or testing. Changes are made to solve real problems or reduce risk, not to add unnecessary complexity.

### Schedule Discipline

We commit to design freeze points (architecture, geometry, frame, systems). After a freeze, changes require a clear reason such as a rule conflict, safety concern, or demonstrated failure.

### Definition of Done

A system is complete when it functions as intended, can be assembled and serviced, has understood limitations, and is documented well enough for others to follow.

### Safety & Conduct

Safety overrides schedule and performance. Unsafe work stops immediately. Respectful, professional communication is expected at all times.

### Documentation & Knowledge Transfer

We document design intent, test results, failures, fixes, and lessons learned so future teams can build on our work.

### Commitment

Completing a reliable car is success. Learning is a core outcome. Clear communication matters as much as technical skill.
